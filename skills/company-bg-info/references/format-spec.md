# BG Info — Format specification

This document defines the visual style for all BG-info Word documents.

## Typography

| Element | Font | Size | Weight | Colour |
|---------|------|------|--------|--------|
| Document title | Schibsted Grotesk | 14 pt | Bold | `#333333` |
| Main company heading | Schibsted Grotesk | 18 pt | Bold | `#333333` |
| Body text | Schibsted Grotesk | 10 pt | Regular | `#333333` |
| Section headings (H1/H2) | Schibsted Grotesk | 10 pt | Bold | `#617D24` (olive green) |
| Table header | Schibsted Grotesk | 9 pt | Bold | `#333333` |
| Table body | Schibsted Grotesk | 9 pt | Regular | `#333333` |
| Image caption | Schibsted Grotesk | 8 pt | Italic | `#666666` |
| Hyperlink | Schibsted Grotesk | 10 pt | Regular | `#0563C1` (blue), underlined |

## Paragraph settings

- Body line spacing: 1.5
- Section heading space before: 12 pt
- Section heading space after: 6 pt
- Body paragraph space after: 6 pt
- No extra space before body paragraphs

## Page setup

- Size: A4 (210 × 297 mm)
- Margins: 1.0 in (25.4 mm) all sides

## Tables

- Style: `Table Grid`
- Alignment: centre
- Header row: shaded `#E5E5E5`
- Vertical alignment: centre
- No extra borders beyond grid

## Images

- Alignment: centre
- Width: ~5.5 in (140 mm) for full-width slides
- Caption: italic, 8 pt, `#666666`, centred below image
- Logo (if used): top-right, ~1.2 in wide

## Hyperlinks

- Inline hyperlinks: blue `#0563C1`, underlined, placed at the claim
- Sources section: same URLs repeated as bulleted list
- If URL is very long, use a short label in the Sources section

## Notes

- Do **not** make headings dramatically larger than body text (10 pt max).
- Do **not** use decorative colours beyond the palette above.
- Keep analysis minimal; present facts, tables, and a one-paragraph collaboration hook.
