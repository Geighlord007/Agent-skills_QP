# Cosmetics Sub-Mode

Activate when the topic is a cosmetic ingredient, raw material, or beauty product claim. This sub-mode extends the generic methodology with cosmetics-specific threads and data sources.

## Extra threads to consider

| ID | Thread | Focus |
|----|--------|-------|
| C1 | INCI & CAS | Official ingredient identity, synonyms |
| C2 | Formulation compatibility | pH, temperature, co-formulants, stability |
| C3 | Consumer perception | Reviews, social listening, claims |
| C4 | Retail & pricing | SKUs, channels, price points |

## Data sources

| Database | URL | Use |
|----------|-----|-----|
| CosIng | `https://ec.europa.eu/growth/tools-databases/cosing/` | EU regulatory status |
| NMPA 已使用化妆品原料目录 | `https://www.nmpa.gov.cn/` | China positive list |
| EWG Skin Deep | `https://www.ewg.org/skindeep/` | Hazard scoring |
| Incidecoder | `https://incidecoder.com/` | Ingredient profiles |
| MFDS / KCA | official sites | Korea regulatory status |

## Special rules

- Brand efficacy claims must be labeled "source: brand/interest-conflicted".
- Hard facts (regulatory, patent, clinical) must come from official or peer-reviewed sources.
- Include the five key markets: China, EU, US, Korea, Japan.
