# Report Template

Use this as a starting outline. Remove or rename sections based on the threads that produced data. Every section must contain narrative text; tables supplement, not replace, prose.

## 1. Executive Summary

- 3-5 bullet takeaways, each with a key number or judgment.
- One-paragraph synthesis: technical feasibility, key risks, recommended next step.
- Flag any unresolved conflicts with `⚠️`.

## 2. Background & Fundamentals

- Identity, source, structure, nomenclature.
- Why this topic matters for the user's stated purpose.
- Key definitions needed to read the rest of the report.

## 3. Mechanism / Biology

- Biochemical or physiological mechanism.
- Structure-function relationships.
- Known modifications (PTM, mutations, formulation) that affect activity.

## 4. Production & Engineering Options

- Expression hosts, strains, or processes.
- Engineering strategies with trade-offs.
- Downstream or formulation considerations.

## 5. Safety, Toxicology & Regulatory

- Available safety data and evidence quality.
- Regulatory status by jurisdiction.
- Pathway to approval / compliance (GRAS, Novel Food, IND, cosmetic ingredient, etc.).

## 6. Clinical / Functional Evidence

- Relevant studies, assays, or clinical data.
- Effect sizes, sample sizes, limitations.
- Gaps in evidence.

## 7. Patent & Freedom-to-Operate

- Key patents, assignees, claims of interest.
- Patent expiry or pending status.
- FTO risks and mitigations.

## 8. Competitive Landscape

- Key companies / products / platforms.
- Differentiation.
- Partnerships and funding if relevant.

## 9. Recommendations & Next Steps

- Prioritized action items.
- Experiments, filings, or analyses to perform next.
- Decision criteria and stop/go points.

## 10. Open Questions & Data Gaps

- Numbered list of unresolved questions.
- Suggested method to resolve each.

## 11. References

- All sources cited in the report, numbered consistently.
- Each entry includes: ID, type (paper/patent/official/news), title/description, URL/PMID/patent number, accessed date.
